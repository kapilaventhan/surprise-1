# Romantic Birthday Surprise Website ❤️

A luxury, modern, fully responsive romantic birthday website created with smooth 60 FPS animations, glassmorphic styling, and an emotional love story atmosphere.

## 📁 Directory Structure & Media Setup

To personalize the website with your own photos, music, and video files, place your files in the corresponding folders under `public/`:

```
/public/
├── images/     <-- Place your romantic photos & memory timeline images here
├── music/      <-- Place your background music file (e.g., song.mp3) here
├── video/      <-- Place your birthday video message (e.g., message.mp4) here
└── assets/     <-- Place any extra icons, graphics, or branding assets here
```

### 1. Photos
- Add your photos into `public/images/`.
- You can also upload photos directly on the live website using the **Photo Gallery** "Add Your Photo" button.

### 2. Music
- Place your background MP3 song inside `public/music/song.mp3`.
- The website also includes an automated Web Audio API romantic piano synthesizer that plays ambient lullaby music automatically if no custom MP3 is specified!

### 3. Video
- Place your special video message inside `public/video/birthday.mp4`.
- You can also upload video files directly on the page in the **Video Section**.

## ✨ Key Features Included

- **Loading Screen**: Animated beating heart with "A little surprise is waiting..."
- **Hero Section**: Typewriter animation, "Happy Birthday ❤️ [Her Name]", "Begin the Journey" button
- **Music Controls**: Floating player with play/pause, volume slider, and track toggle
- **Glass Countdown**: Countdown timer to August 8 (or custom date) with fireworks & confetti burst
- **3D Love Letter**: Animated envelope that unfolds into a handwritten live typing letter
- **Memory Timeline**: Vertical sliding cards detailing key milestones
- **Masonry Photo Gallery**: Image lightbox with captions & photo upload capability
- **12 Reasons Why I Love You**: 3D interactive flip cards
- **Love Quotes Carousel**: Auto-rotating romantic quote slider
- **🎁 Surprise Gift Button**: Full-screen celebration with glowing heart rain & fireworks
- **Video Section**: Responsive player for personal video messages
- **Floating Love Notes**: Toast popups appearing every 10 seconds randomly
- **Cursor Effects**: Glowing heart mouse trail with sparkle particle effects
- **Final Night Sky Ending**: Night backdrop with moon, glowing heart, and ending pledge ("I will always choose you.")
- **Personalization Panel**: Edit partner's name, your name, birthday date, and letter text on the fly!
